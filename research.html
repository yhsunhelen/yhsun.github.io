Research

My work spans world model evaluation, learning through physical interaction, and sensing systems. The projects below describe my current research at the University of Washington.

World modelsSensingImmersive HCI

SafeWorld: Temporal Safety Verification for Learned World Models

Apr 2026–Present

University of Washington · Advisor: Luyao Niu

I combine action-conditioned latent predictions with LTL and STL specifications to evaluate safety in learned world models. Using SafeDreamer checkpoints, I compare predicted trajectory safety with actual environment behavior.

Benchmarking World Model Reliability Under Environmental Perturbations

Sep 2026–Present

University of Washington · Advisor: Luyao Niu

I develop a multi-environment benchmark that compares predicted and ground-truth trajectories under matched initial states and action sequences. Controlled perturbations help characterize prediction failures, generalization, and false-safe assessments.

Continual Spatial World Models from Physical Experience

May 2026–Present

University of Washington · Independent research

I investigate how experience supports continual spatial learning in a simulated dynamic maze. Spatial Change Memory learns relations that transfer to an unseen spatial transition, while state–action–outcome updates refine predictions within a known physical model.

Auxetic Structures for Robotic Tactile Sensing

Sep 2026–Present

University of Washington · UbiComp Lab · Mentor: Alex Ching

I design and evaluate auxetic structures for flexible robotic sensing surfaces, studying how geometry affects deformation, conformability, and integration with contact sensors.

Capacitive Insole Sensing for Foot Pressure Measurement

Jul 2026–Present

University of Washington · UbiComp Lab · Mentor: Alex Ching

I conduct preliminary capacitive sensing experiments and develop insole models to investigate sensor placement, mechanical deformation, and integration into wearable pressure sensing systems.

Attention and Mind Wandering in Virtual Reality Classrooms

Mar 2026–Present

University of Washington · NEWT Lab · Mentor: Jiexin Ding

I build Unity classroom environments and contribute to pilot studies using Meta Quest Pro eye tracking and behavioral data to study attention, mind wandering, and learning performance.
