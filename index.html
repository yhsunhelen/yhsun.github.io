About me

I am a master's student in Technology Innovation (Robotics Track) at the University of Washington. I work on reliable world models and embodied learning, with a focus on evaluating learned predictions and understanding how agents acquire knowledge through physical interaction.

At UW, I work with Luyao Niu on world model safety and evaluation, with Alex Ching in the UbiComp Lab on tactile and wearable sensing, and with Jiexin Ding in the NEWT Lab on attention in VR. I previously studied Product Design at Wuhan University.
